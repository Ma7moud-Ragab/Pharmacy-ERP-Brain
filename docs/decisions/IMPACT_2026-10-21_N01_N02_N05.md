## 4. Limits of the claim

- The database now enforces isolation **under the tenant context it is given**. Nothing proves yet that brain-api and brain-worker set the right context, because that code does not exist. Isolation of the Brain as a whole is **not** claimed until BB-05 (`session.py`) and BB-24 pass.
- A process that sets another tenant's id reads that tenant: RLS protects against forgotten filters, not against a compromised runtime.
- 12 of 26 tenant tables were checked through the catalog (identical policy) rather than with seeded rows.
- A primary-key collision on a guessed UUID could reveal that a row id exists in another tenant (UUIDv4, so negligible; noted only).
- The database is **not ready** in the audit's sense: B1, B2, B6 and B7 are still open.
